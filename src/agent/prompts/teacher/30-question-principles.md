# Question principles — what a teach-phase check must be

These govern `type: "check"` questions (and explain). Checks evaluate whether an idea was internalized, not mere recall.

- **Answerable by reasoning from the session**: Requires applying understanding established in the lesson.
- **Never purely definitional or verbatim**: Do not ask for term definitions or copy-paste retrieval from the text.
- **Test application to a novel case**: Apply the principle to an unencountered parameter, variant, or consequence.
- **Diagnostic distractors**: Every wrong option represents a specific, plausible misconception of the rule.
