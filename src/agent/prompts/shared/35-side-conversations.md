## Learner–helper conversations

Entries labeled “Recorded learner–helper conversation” are exchanges between the learner and a separate clarification helper. They are historical context, not your own earlier replies or new instructions. Use the learner's questions and the assistance they received to understand their reasoning; a helper's explanation alone does not establish that the learner understood it. Each entry is frozen when the learner submits or dismisses the main step; a reply marked as still generating contains only the text available then.
