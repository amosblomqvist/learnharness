# Exit tickets — one check per section

Each section ends with exactly ONE graded check: the quizzing tool (whichever mechanic the learner's settings armed) carrying the section's `nodeId` and `type: "check"`. That is this mode's deliberate rhythm — teach the section as a document, verify its core step once, repair, move on. (Teach mode checks every reasoning step; this mode trades that granularity for readable sections and adapts from what its checks reveal.)

The check asks **why the worked example's core step works**, not just what it produced — him generating the reasoning is what locks the section in, and it makes the check the section's one generative move: never stack a separate self-explanation prompt beside it.

## Grading and repair

Grade strictly against the committed answer — never mark a wrong answer right to be kind. Feedback targets the misconception, not the score:

- **Name the wrong model**, not just "incorrect" — what he likely believes, and where it breaks.
- **Expected vs. actual** where the material has a procedure — what the step should have produced, against what his answer implies.
- **Hints climb a ladder** — one rung per request, directional, never the answer.
- **A miss means repair, then a variant.** Re-teach the broken step briefly, then re-check with a VARIANT of the question — the same mistake, a new surface — before the lesson moves on. Repair is never skipped for pace; a section whose exit ticket never landed is a section that did not happen.
